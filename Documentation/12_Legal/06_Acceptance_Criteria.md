# SMEvolve Legal Specification
## Part 6 – Acceptance Criteria

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Legal Module Acceptance Criteria

---

# Purpose

This document defines the final requirements that must be satisfied before the SMEvolve Legal module is considered complete.

It ensures legal pages meet content, design, accessibility, SEO, security, and usability standards.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before marking the Legal module complete:

1. Review all documents inside Documentation/12_Legal/.
2. Verify Global Features are reused.
3. Verify legal pages use the shared architecture.
4. Confirm no duplicated legal layouts exist.

---

# Required Pages

The Legal module must include:

✓ Privacy Policy

✓ Terms & Conditions

✓ Cookie Policy

---

# Page Architecture

Verify:

✓ All legal pages use the same layout.

✓ Hero section is reusable.

✓ Footer is consistent.

✓ Breadcrumbs are implemented.

✓ Content structure is consistent.

---

# Privacy Policy Requirements

Verify:

✓ Data collection is explained.

✓ Contact form data is covered.

✓ Quote Wizard data is covered.

✓ Cookie usage is referenced.

✓ User rights are explained.

✓ Contact information exists.

---

# Terms & Conditions Requirements

Verify:

✓ Website usage rules exist.

✓ Service terms are explained.

✓ Quote process is covered.

✓ Payment expectations are explained.

✓ Client responsibilities are defined.

✓ Intellectual property considerations exist.

---

# Cookie Policy Requirements

Verify:

✓ Cookies are explained.

✓ Cookie categories are documented.

✓ Analytics usage is covered.

✓ Third-party cookies are explained.

✓ Cookie management is described.

The Legal module is complete when:

- All legal pages are implemented.

- Accessibility requirements are met.
- Responsive behaviour is verified.
- Security requirements are satisfied.
