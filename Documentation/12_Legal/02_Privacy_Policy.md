# Purpose

This document defines the structure and requirements for the SMEvolve Privacy Policy page.

The Privacy Policy explains how SMEvolve collects, uses, protects, and manages personal information provided by website visitors, prospective clients, and future platform users.

The document should build trust by clearly explaining data practices in simple language.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this page:

1. Read Legal Overview.
2. Read Global Features documentation.
3. Reuse the shared Legal Page layout.
4. Do not create a separate privacy page architecture.

Only the content should differ.

---

# Business Goals

The Privacy Policy should:

- Build user confidence.
- Demonstrate responsible data handling.
- Support business transparency.
- Prepare for future digital services.

---

# User Goals

Visitors should understand:

- What information SMEvolve collects.
- Why information is collected.
- How information is protected.
- Their rights regarding personal data.

# Content Sections

---

# 1. Introduction

Explain:

- SMEvolve respects user privacy.
- This policy explains data practices.
- Users should understand how information is handled.

---

# 2. Information We Collect

Include:

## Information Provided Directly

Examples:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Company information
- Project details submitted through forms

---

## Automatically Collected Information

Examples:

- Browser information
- Device information
- IP address
- Website usage data
- Cookies

---

# 3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for:

- Responding to enquiries.
- Preparing project proposals.
- Improving services.
- Communicating with users.
- Website analytics.
- Security monitoring.

---

# 4. Quote Wizard Data

Explain:

Information submitted through the Quote Wizard may include:

- Business details
- Project requirements
- Budget information
- Timeline expectations

Purpose:

To prepare suitable recommendations and proposals.

---

# 5. Contact Form Data

Explain:

Contact information is used only for:

- Responding to enquiries.
- Providing requested information.
- Continuing business discussions.

---

# 6. Cookies & Tracking

Explain:

SMEvolve may use cookies for:

- Website functionality.
- Analytics.
- Performance improvements.
- User experience improvements.

Link:

Cookie Policy

---

# 7. Third-Party Services

Potential services:

- Analytics platforms
- Email providers
- Hosting providers
- Payment providers (future)

Explain:

Third-party providers process information according to their own policies.

---

# 8. Data Protection

Explain security practices:

- Secure hosting.
- Access control.
- Input validation.
- Secure communication.
- Regular maintenance.

Avoid claiming absolute security.

---

# 9. User Rights

Users may have rights including:

- Requesting information.
- Requesting corrections.
- Requesting deletion where applicable.
- Withdrawing communication preferences.

---

# 10. Data Retention

Explain:

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for:

- Business communication.
- Legal obligations.
- Service delivery.

---

# 11. Children's Privacy

State:

SMEvolve services are intended for businesses and users capable of entering professional agreements.

---

# 12. Policy Updates

Explain:

The Privacy Policy may change as services evolve.

The updated date should be displayed clearly.
