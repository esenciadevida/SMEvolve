# Purpose

This document defines the structure and implementation requirements for the SMEvolve Terms & Conditions page.

The Terms & Conditions explain the rules for using the SMEvolve website, engaging with services, requesting quotations, and entering business relationships with SMEvolve.

The document should establish clear expectations between SMEvolve and website visitors or clients.

# Content Sections

---

# 1. Introduction

Explain:

- These terms govern website usage and services.
- By using the website, users agree to these terms.

---

# 2. Website Usage

Define:

Users should:

- Use the website lawfully.
- Avoid harmful activities.
- Not attempt unauthorized access.
- Not misuse website content.

---

# 3. Services

Explain:

SMEvolve provides technology solutions including:

- Website Development
- Software Development
- AI Solutions
- Automation
- Digital Services

Services are subject to agreed project requirements.

---

# 4. Quotations

Explain:

Quotes provided by SMEvolve:

- Are based on provided project information.
- May change if requirements change.
- Are valid for a defined period.

---

# 5. Project Agreements

Explain:

Before starting work:

- Scope should be confirmed.
- Deliverables should be agreed.
- Timelines should be established.
- Payment terms should be accepted.

---

# 6. Payments

Explain:

Payment terms may include:

- Deposits.
- Milestone payments.
- Final payments.

Specific terms depend on the agreed project agreement.

---

# 7. Project Delivery

Explain:

SMEvolve aims to:

- Deliver agreed work.
- Communicate progress.
- Maintain quality standards.

Timelines may change due to:

- Requirement changes.
- Client delays.
- External dependencies.

---

# 8. Client Responsibilities

Clients should provide:

- Accurate information.
- Required content.
- Timely feedback.
- Necessary approvals.

---

# 9. Intellectual Property

Explain:

Ownership transfer depends on:

- Project agreement.
- Payment completion.
- Third-party licenses.

SMEvolve retains rights to reusable:

- Frameworks.
- Tools.
- Development methods.
- Internal systems.

---

# 10. Third-Party Services

Explain:

Projects may use:

- Hosting providers.
- Payment providers.
- APIs.
- External software.

Third-party services are governed by their own terms.

---

# 11. Website Content

Explain:

Website information may include:

- Service descriptions.
- Pricing information.
- Technical information.

SMEvolve may update content without notice.

---

# 12. Limitation of Liability

Explain:

SMEvolve provides services professionally but cannot guarantee:

- Continuous website availability.
- Third-party platform performance.
- External service availability.

---

# 13. Policy Updates

Explain:

Terms may change as:

- Services evolve.
- Business requirements change.
- Legal requirements change.

Updated dates should be displayed.
