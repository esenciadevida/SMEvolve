# 1. Introduction

Explain:

- SMEvolve uses cookies to improve website functionality.
- Users can manage cookie preferences.

---

# 2. What Are Cookies?

Explain:

Cookies are small text files stored on a user's device that help websites remember information and understand usage patterns.

---

# 3. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies may help with:

- Website functionality.
- Security.
- Performance.
- Analytics.
- User experience improvement.

---

# 4. Types of Cookies

Organize cookies into categories.

---

## Essential Cookies

Purpose:

Required for website operation.

Examples:

- Session management.
- Security features.
- Form functionality.

These cannot usually be disabled.

---

## Performance Cookies

Purpose:

Understand website usage.

Examples:

- Analytics.
- Page performance monitoring.

---

## Functional Cookies

Purpose:

Remember user preferences.

Examples:

- Theme preference.
- Language preference.

---

## Marketing Cookies

Future use.

Purpose:

Measure campaigns and improve marketing.

Examples:

- Advertising analytics.
- Conversion tracking.

---

# 5. Third-Party Cookies

Explain:

Third-party services may place cookies depending on integrations.

Examples:

- Analytics providers.
- Embedded content.
- Payment services.

Users should review third-party policies.

---

# 6. Cookie Management

Explain users can:

- Adjust browser settings.
- Disable cookies.
- Clear existing cookies.

Note:

Disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

---

# 7. Cookie Duration

Explain cookies may be:

## Session Cookies

Deleted after browser closure.

## Persistent Cookies

Remain until expiration or manual deletion.

---

# 8. Future Cookie Consent System

Architecture should support future implementation of:

- Cookie banner.
- Preference management.
- Consent logging.
- Category controls.
