# SMEvolve Homepage Content Architecture
## Final CTA Section Specification

**Project:** SMEvolve Website

**Section:** Homepage Final Call To Action

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Final CTA Section is the final conversion point on the SMEvolve homepage.

Its purpose is to encourage visitors who are interested in SMEvolve's services to take action.

The section should make starting a conversation feel simple and accessible.

---

# Section Goals

The Final CTA should:

- Encourage project enquiries.
- Reduce hesitation.
- Provide clear next steps.
- Convert interested visitors into leads.

---

# Position in Homepage

The section appears after:

```
FAQ

↓

Final CTA

↓

Footer
```

---

# User Questions Answered

## "What should I do next?"

Answer:

Start a conversation with SMEvolve.

---

## "How can I discuss my project?"

Answer:

Request a quote or contact SMEvolve directly.

---

# Design Requirements

The section should:

- Have strong contrast.
- Highlight the primary action.
- Avoid unnecessary content.
- Create urgency without pressure.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop:

Horizontal CTA layout.

Mobile:

Stacked buttons.

The primary CTA should appear first.

---

# Accessibility Requirements

Must include:

- Accessible buttons.
- Clear labels.
- Proper contrast.
- Keyboard navigation.

---

# SEO Considerations

The section should naturally reinforce:

- Web development services.
- Software development.
- Digital solutions.
- Business technology.

Avoid keyword stuffing.
