# SMEvolve Homepage Content Architecture
## Why Choose SMEvolve Section Specification

**Project:** SMEvolve Website

**Section:** Homepage Why Choose SMEvolve

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Why Choose SMEvolve Section explains the company's unique value proposition.

While the Services Section explains **what SMEvolve does**, this section explains:

- Why SMEvolve is different.
- Why businesses should trust SMEvolve.
- Why SMEvolve is a suitable technology partner.

---

# Section Goals

The section should:

- Differentiate SMEvolve from competitors.
- Communicate business benefits.
- Build confidence.
- Address common client concerns.
- Encourage visitors toward conversion.

---

# Position in Homepage

The section appears after:

```
Hero Section

↓

Trust Section

↓

Services Section

↓

Why Choose SMEvolve

↓

Technology Stack
```

# User Questions Answered

## "Why should I work with SMEvolve?"

Answer:

Because SMEvolve combines technical expertise with business understanding to create solutions that deliver real value.

---

## "Will they understand my business?"

Answer:

SMEvolve focuses on understanding business challenges before recommending technology solutions.

---

## "Will the solution grow with my business?"

Answer:

Solutions are built with scalability and future improvement in mind.

# Design Requirements

The section should:

- Focus on benefits, not technical jargon.
- Use clear and simple language.
- Maintain strong visual hierarchy.
- Feel professional and trustworthy.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop:

Use grid layout.

Tablet:

Two-column layout.

Mobile:

Single-column stacked cards.

---

# Accessibility Requirements

Must include:

- Semantic headings.
- Accessible icons.
- Clear readable text.
- Proper contrast.
