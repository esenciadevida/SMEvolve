# SMEvolve Homepage Content Architecture
## Trust & Credibility Section Specification

**Project:** SMEvolve Website

**Section:** Homepage Trust Section

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Trust Section exists to reduce visitor hesitation and establish confidence in SMEvolve as a reliable technology partner.

Many potential clients visiting the website may be:

- Business owners unfamiliar with software development.
- Startups looking for technical partners.
- Companies comparing multiple providers.

This section provides immediate credibility before introducing detailed services.

---

# Trust Section Goals

The section should:

- Build confidence.
- Demonstrate expertise.
- Highlight professionalism.
- Show SMEvolve's commitment to quality.
- Encourage visitors to continue exploring.

---

# Position in Homepage

The Trust Section appears directly after:

```
Hero Section
        ↓
Trust Section
        ↓
Services Section
```

---

# User Questions Answered

The section should answer:

## "Can I trust this company?"

Answer:

SMEvolve follows professional development practices and builds reliable digital solutions.

---

## "Do they understand modern technology?"

Answer:

SMEvolve works with current development technologies and scalable approaches.

---

## "Will they understand my business?"

Answer:

SMEvolve focuses on solving business problems, not only writing code.

# Design Requirements

The section should:

- Use consistent spacing.
- Maintain visual simplicity.
- Avoid overwhelming visitors.
- Match the SMEvolve design system.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop:

Display cards horizontally.

Tablet:

Use two-column grid.

Mobile:

Stack cards vertically.

---

# Accessibility Requirements

- Proper heading hierarchy.
- Meaningful icons.
- Text alternatives where required.
- Keyboard accessibility.
