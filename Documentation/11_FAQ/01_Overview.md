# Purpose

This document defines the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) page for the SMEvolve website.

The FAQ page serves as a central knowledge base that answers common questions about SMEvolve's services, processes, pricing, project delivery, support, and engagement model.

It should reduce repetitive enquiries, improve user confidence, and support lead generation by directing visitors toward the Contact page or Quote Wizard when additional assistance is required.

The FAQ page also acts as the shared source for FAQ sections displayed throughout the website, such as those on the Homepage, Services, Packages, and Contact pages.

# Business Goals

The FAQ page should:

- Reduce support enquiries.

- Encourage visitors to continue their journey through the website.
