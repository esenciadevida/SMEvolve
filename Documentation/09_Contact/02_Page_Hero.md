# SMEvolve Contact Page Specification
## Part 2 – Page Hero

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Contact Page Hero Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the hero section for the SMEvolve Contact page.

The hero should immediately reassure visitors that reaching out is simple, welcoming, and the next logical step in starting a project with SMEvolve.

Unlike the Homepage hero, this section should focus on communication rather than marketing.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the Contact Overview.
3. Read the Design System.
4. Reuse the global Hero component.

Do not create a separate hero implementation.

---

# Business Goals

The hero should:

- Encourage visitors to contact SMEvolve.
- Reinforce trust.
- Reduce hesitation.
- Clearly explain what happens next.

---

# User Goals

Visitors should immediately understand:

- They're in the right place.
- SMEvolve welcomes enquiries.
- Multiple communication methods are available.
- Responses are prompt and professional.

---

# Layout

The hero should contain:

- Breadcrumb Navigation
- Small Label
- Primary Heading
- Supporting Paragraph
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Supporting Illustration

---

# Small Label

Suggested:

CONTACT SMEVOLVE

---

# Primary Heading

Suggested:

Let's Build Something Great Together

Alternative options:

- Let's Talk About Your Next Project
- We'd Love to Hear From You
- Tell Us About Your Business Goals

---

# Supporting Paragraph

Example:

Whether you're planning a new website, custom software, AI-powered solution, or simply have a question, our team is ready to help. Reach out using your preferred method and we'll respond as quickly as possible.

---

# Primary CTA

Label:

Get a Free Quote

Destination:

/quote

---

# Secondary CTA

Label:

Chat on WhatsApp

This should open a WhatsApp conversation when configured.

---

# Hero Illustration

Use a modern illustration or abstract graphic representing:

- Communication
- Collaboration
- Customer Support
- Business Partnership

Avoid generic stock photography.

---

# Trust Indicators

Display a small row beneath the CTAs.

Examples:

✓ Fast Response

✓ Friendly Support

✓ No Obligation Consultation

These should reinforce confidence without overwhelming the layout.

---

# Visual Style

The hero should feel:

- Professional
- Open
- Approachable
- Spacious

Maintain consistency with the Homepage hero.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop

- Two-column layout
- Text on the left
- Illustration on the right

Mobile

- Single-column layout
- Text first
- Illustration below

CTAs should remain easy to tap.

---

# Accessibility

The hero must:

- Maintain semantic heading structure.
- Include meaningful alternative text for illustrations.
- Provide keyboard-accessible CTA buttons.
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical.

---

# SEO

The hero should contain the page H1.

Only one H1 should appear on the page.

---

# Analytics

Track:

- Hero Quote CTA clicks.
- Hero WhatsApp clicks.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The hero is complete when:

- Messaging is welcoming.
- CTAs are prominent.
- Illustration reinforces communication.
- Trust indicators are displayed.
- Responsive behaviour is verified.
- Accessibility requirements are met.

---

# Revision Policy

Future messaging improvements should preserve the overall layout and conversion-focused purpose of the Contact page hero.
