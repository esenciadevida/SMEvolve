# SMEvolve Contact Page Specification
## Part 8 – Final Call To Action

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Final Call To Action Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the final Call To Action (CTA) section displayed at the bottom of the Contact page.

The objective is to encourage visitors who have reached the end of the page to take the next step by contacting SMEvolve or requesting a quote.

The CTA should feel supportive and motivating rather than overly promotional.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the Contact Overview.
3. Read the Design System.
4. Reuse the global Final CTA component where appropriate.

Avoid creating a unique CTA component if an existing reusable component satisfies the requirements.

---

# Business Goals

This section should:

- Increase enquiries.
- Increase quote requests.
- Reinforce confidence.
- Provide a final opportunity for conversion.

---

# User Goals

Visitors should leave this page knowing:

- SMEvolve is ready to help.
- Starting a conversation is simple.
- There is no pressure or obligation.

---

# Layout

The section should contain:

- Small Label
- Headline
- Supporting Paragraph
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Optional Trust Indicators

The content should remain centered with generous spacing.

---

# Small Label

Suggested:

READY TO GET STARTED?

---

# Heading

Suggested:

Let's Turn Your Ideas Into Reality

Alternative options:

- Let's Build Your Next Digital Solution
- Ready to Start Your Project?
- Let's Create Something Exceptional Together

---

# Supporting Paragraph

Example:

Whether you're planning a new website, custom software, AI-powered solution, or simply exploring your options, we're here to help. Reach out today and let's discuss how SMEvolve can support your business goals.

---

# Primary CTA

Label:

Request a Free Quote

Destination:

/quote

This should be the primary conversion action.

---

# Secondary CTA

Label:

Chat on WhatsApp

Open the configured WhatsApp conversation.

---

# Optional Trust Indicators

Display a short list such as:

✓ Free initial consultation

✓ Transparent communication

✓ Tailored business solutions

✓ Ongoing project support

Keep the messaging concise.

---

# Visual Design

The CTA should:

- Match the Homepage CTA style.
- Use the primary brand colours.
- Include generous whitespace.
- Draw attention without overwhelming the visitor.

Avoid excessive animations.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop

Centered layout with horizontally aligned buttons where space permits.

Mobile

Stack buttons vertically with full-width tap targets.

---

# Accessibility

The section must:

- Maintain proper heading hierarchy.
- Provide descriptive button labels.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Include visible focus indicators.
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical.

---

# Analytics

Track:

- Primary CTA clicks.
- Secondary CTA clicks.
- CTA impressions (optional future enhancement).

---

# Components

Reuse:

- Global Final CTA component.
- Global Button components.
- Global Section layout.

Avoid creating page-specific CTA implementations unless additional functionality is required.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The section is complete when:

- Messaging is encouraging and clear.
- CTA buttons function correctly.
- Responsive behaviour is verified.
- Accessibility requirements are met.
- Shared components are reused.
- Analytics events are identified.

---

# Revision Policy

Future marketing campaigns may customise CTA messaging while preserving the overall structure and component architecture.
