# SMEvolve About Page Specification
## Part 2 – Hero & Company Overview

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** About Page Section Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Hero and Company Overview sections of the SMEvolve About page.

These sections should immediately introduce SMEvolve, communicate its purpose, and establish trust by explaining what the company does and the value it delivers.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the PRD.
3. Read the Information Architecture.
4. Read the Design System.
5. Read the Brand Messaging document.
6. Read the About Master Specification.
7. Follow this document exactly.

Do not invent company history, achievements, client numbers, awards, or years of experience unless they are documented elsewhere.

---

# Business Goal

Introduce SMEvolve as a reliable technology partner that helps businesses grow through modern digital solutions.

Visitors should immediately understand who SMEvolve is and why the company exists.

---

# User Goal

After reading this section, visitors should know:

- What SMEvolve does.
- Who SMEvolve helps.
- What makes the company different.
- Why they should continue exploring the website.

---

# Section Placement

This section appears at the top of the About page.

It should be followed by:

- Our Story

---

# Section Structure

The section should contain:

1. Hero
2. Company Overview
3. Primary CTA
4. Secondary CTA

---

# Hero Heading

Use:

**Building Digital Solutions That Help Businesses Grow**

---

# Hero Supporting Text

Use:

> SMEvolve partners with businesses to design, build, and maintain modern digital solutions that improve efficiency, strengthen online presence, and support long-term growth.

---

# Company Overview

Introduce SMEvolve as a technology company focused on solving business challenges through practical, scalable, and reliable software solutions.

Highlight that the company delivers tailored solutions rather than one-size-fits-all products.

Avoid technical jargon and focus on business outcomes.

---

# Primary CTA

Button Text:

**Get a Free Quote**

Destination:

```
/quote
```

---

# Secondary CTA

Button Text:

**Explore Our Services**

Destination:

```
/services
```

---

# Design Requirements

The Hero should:

- Use a strong visual hierarchy.
- Include generous whitespace.
- Be readable on all screen sizes.
- Align with the Homepage visual style.

The Company Overview should:

- Use short paragraphs.
- Maintain comfortable reading width.
- Transition naturally into the "Our Story" section.

---

# Accessibility

Ensure:

- Only one H1 exists on the page.
- Buttons are keyboard accessible.
- Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
- Images include meaningful alt text where appropriate.

---

# SEO Considerations

Primary keyword themes:

- About SMEvolve
- Technology Company
- Digital Solutions
- Software Development

Write naturally.

Do not over-optimize.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This section is complete when:

- The Hero clearly introduces SMEvolve.
- The Company Overview explains the business simply.
- Both CTA buttons are present.
- The layout is responsive.
- Accessibility requirements are met.
- The section follows the Design System.

# Revision Policy

Future updates may refine messaging but should remain aligned with the Brand Messaging and About Master Specification.
