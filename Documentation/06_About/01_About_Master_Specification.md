# SMEvolve About Page Specification
## Part 1 – About Master Specification

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** About Page Master Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the objectives, structure, content strategy, design principles, and implementation standards for the SMEvolve About page.

The About page exists to introduce SMEvolve as a trusted technology partner, communicate the company's mission and values, explain its approach to solving business problems, and strengthen visitor confidence.

This document serves as the master reference for all About page content and implementation.

---

# Business Goal

The About page should build trust and credibility by helping visitors understand:

- Who SMEvolve is.
- Why the company exists.
- What it believes in.
- How it works.
- Why businesses should choose SMEvolve.

The page should encourage visitors to continue exploring the website or start a conversation.

---

# Primary Objectives

The About page should:

- Introduce SMEvolve clearly.
- Communicate the company's mission and vision.
- Explain the company's approach to digital solutions.
- Reinforce trust through transparency.
- Support conversions with clear calls to action.

---

# Target Audience

Primary Audience

- Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)
- Startups
- Entrepreneurs

Secondary Audience

- Schools
- Hospitals
- NGOs
- Retail Businesses
- Professional Service Firms
- Government and Non-Profit Organizations

---

# User Intent

Visitors coming to the About page typically want to know:

- Who is SMEvolve?
- Can this company be trusted?
- What makes SMEvolve different?
- What experience or philosophy does the company have?
- Should I work with SMEvolve?

The page should answer these questions naturally.

---

# Page Structure

Every About page should follow this structure.

1. Hero
2. Company Overview
3. Our Story
4. Mission, Vision & Values
5. Why Choose SMEvolve
6. Meet the Team (or Company Philosophy if no public team profiles)
7. Final Call To Action

---

# Navigation

The About page should be accessible from:

- Primary Navigation
- Footer
- Homepage
- Internal links throughout the website

---

# Conversion Strategy

Primary Conversion

- Get a Free Quote

Secondary Conversions

- Contact Us
- Book a Consultation
- Chat on WhatsApp

The page should encourage action without interrupting the visitor's reading experience.

---

# Key Messages

Visitors should leave the About page understanding that:

- SMEvolve is committed to solving business problems through technology.
- Every project is tailored to the client's needs.
- Long-term relationships are valued over one-time projects.
- Quality, transparency, and reliability are core principles.

---

# Design Principles

The About page should feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Honest
- Friendly
- Trustworthy

Use generous whitespace, readable typography, and consistent spacing.

---

# Accessibility

The About page must:

- Use semantic HTML.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Include descriptive alt text.
- Maintain proper heading hierarchy.
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical.

---

# SEO Goals

Primary topics include:

- About SMEvolve
- Web Development Company
- Software Development Company
- Digital Solutions
- Technology Partner

Content should prioritize clarity and user intent over keyword density.

---

# Shared Components

Reuse existing Blade components wherever possible.

Examples include:

- Hero section
- Section wrapper
- CTA banner
- Buttons
- Cards
- Icons

Avoid duplicate markup.

---

# Analytics Goals

Track:

- Scroll depth
- CTA clicks
- Contact button clicks
- Quote requests
- WhatsApp clicks

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The About page is complete when:

- The page structure follows this specification.
- Messaging aligns with the Brand Messaging document.
- Accessibility requirements are met.
- SEO principles are followed.
- Calls to action are clearly presented.
- The page supports the overall business goals.

---

# Revision Policy

Future updates should maintain the overall structure while allowing the content to evolve as SMEvolve grows.
