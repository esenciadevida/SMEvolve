# SMEvolve About Page Specification
## Part 6 – Final Call To Action

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** About Page Section Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Final Call To Action (CTA) section of the SMEvolve About page.

The purpose of this section is to encourage visitors who have learned about SMEvolve to take the next step by starting a conversation, requesting a quote, or exploring the company's services.

This section should provide a confident and welcoming conclusion to the About page.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the PRD.
3. Read the Information Architecture.
4. Read the Design System.
5. Read the Brand Messaging document.
6. Read the About Master Specification.
7. Follow this document exactly.

Do not use exaggerated sales language or unrealistic promises.

---

# Business Goal

Convert interested visitors into qualified enquiries by providing a clear and simple next step.

---

# User Goal

Visitors should leave the page knowing:

- SMEvolve is ready to discuss their project.
- Starting the conversation is simple.
- There is no obligation to make an enquiry.

---

# Section Placement

Position this section after:

- Why Choose SMEvolve & Team

This should be the final content section before the global footer.

---

# Section Structure

The section should include:

1. Headline
2. Supporting Message
3. Primary CTA
4. Secondary CTA
5. Trust Note

---

# Headline

Use:

**Let's Build Something That Moves Your Business Forward**

---

# Supporting Message

Use:

> Whether you're planning a new website, custom software, business automation, or another digital solution, SMEvolve is ready to help. Let's discuss your goals and explore the right approach together.

---

# Primary Call To Action

Button Text:

**Get a Free Quote**

Destination:

```
/quote
```

Style:

Primary button.

---

# Secondary Call To Action

Button Text:

**Explore Our Services**

Destination:

```
/services
```

Style:

Secondary button.

---

# Trust Note

Display below the CTA buttons.

Use:

> Every successful project starts with a conversation. We'll listen to your goals, answer your questions, and recommend the solution that best fits your business.

---

# Design Requirements

The section should feel:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Encouraging
- Trustworthy

Use generous whitespace and strong visual hierarchy.

CTA buttons should be immediately visible on all devices.

---

# Accessibility

Ensure:

- Semantic HTML.
- Keyboard accessible buttons.
- Visible focus states.
- Descriptive button labels.
- WCAG AA compliance where practical.

---

# SEO Considerations

The content should naturally reinforce SMEvolve's expertise in delivering digital solutions without excessive keyword repetition.

Prioritize readability and user intent.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The section is complete when:

- The headline and supporting message are displayed.
- Both CTA buttons are present and functional.
- The trust note is included.
- The layout is responsive.
- Accessibility standards are met.
- The design aligns with the Design System.

---

# Revision Policy

Future updates may introduce additional contact options or promotional campaigns, but the primary purpose of this section should remain focused on encouraging meaningful business enquiries.
