# SMEvolve About Page Specification
## Part 5 – Why Choose SMEvolve & Team

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** About Page Section Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the "Why Choose SMEvolve" and "Team" section of the About page.

The purpose of this section is to reinforce trust by highlighting SMEvolve's strengths and introducing the people or philosophy behind the company.

The Team section should be designed to support future expansion without requiring structural changes.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the PRD.
3. Read the Information Architecture.
4. Read the Design System.
5. Read the Brand Messaging document.
6. Read the About Master Specification.
7. Follow this document exactly.

Do not invent employee names, biographies, certifications, awards, or client statistics unless they are documented elsewhere.

---

# Business Goal

Reinforce confidence by explaining why businesses choose SMEvolve and demonstrate the company's commitment to professionalism, collaboration, and long-term support.

---

# User Goal

Visitors should understand:

- What differentiates SMEvolve.
- What they can expect when working with the company.
- That there are real people and a clear philosophy behind every project.

---

# Section Placement

Position this section after:

- Mission, Vision & Values

Position this section before:

- Final Call To Action

---

# Section Structure

The section should include:

1. Why Choose SMEvolve
2. Key Advantages Grid
3. Team / Company Philosophy
4. Transition to Final CTA

---

# Section Heading

Use:

**Why Businesses Choose SMEvolve**

# Team Section

## Heading

**The People Behind SMEvolve**

---

## Current Implementation

If public team profiles are not available, display a company philosophy section explaining that SMEvolve is built around collaboration, continuous learning, and delivering value to every client.

Do not use fictional team members or placeholder biographies.

---

## Future Enhancement

The layout should support adding team member cards without redesigning the page.

Each future card may include:

- Photo
- Name
- Role
- Short biography
- LinkedIn profile (optional)

The component should gracefully display any number of team members.

---

# Design Requirements

The Why Choose section should use a responsive card grid.

The Team section should maintain generous spacing and visual consistency with the rest of the page.

Future team cards should reuse existing card components where appropriate.

---

# Accessibility

Ensure:

- Semantic HTML.
- Accessible headings.
- Keyboard navigation where applicable.
- Meaningful image alt text for future team photos.
- WCAG AA compliance where practical.

---

# SEO Considerations

Naturally reinforce topics including:

- Technology partner
- Software development company
- Digital transformation
- Business solutions

Write naturally and prioritize readability.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The section is complete when:

- All six advantages are displayed.
- The Team / Company Philosophy section is present.
- No fictional information is introduced.
- The layout is responsive.
- The structure supports future team profiles.
- Accessibility standards are met.

---

# Revision Policy

As SMEvolve grows, this section may expand to include leadership profiles, department overviews, company milestones, certifications, or awards without changing the overall page structure.
