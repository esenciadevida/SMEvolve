# Purpose

This document defines the final Call To Action (CTA) section used across all Portfolio pages.

The purpose of this section is to convert visitors who have explored SMEvolve's work into qualified enquiries.

Rather than ending the visitor journey after viewing projects, this section should encourage the next step.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the PRD.
3. Read the Information Architecture.
4. Read the Design System.
5. Read the Brand Messaging document.
6. Read the Portfolio Master Specification.
7. Follow this document exactly.

Do not invent guarantees, timelines, client statistics, or unrealistic promises.

---

# Business Goal

Convert visitors who have viewed portfolio projects into prospective clients.

The CTA should reinforce trust and make the next action clear and simple.

---

# User Goal

After viewing one or more projects, visitors should feel confident enough to:

- Request a quote
- Book a consultation
- Contact SMEvolve
- Start discussing their own project

---

# Core Message

Every successful project starts with a conversation.

Whether you're launching a new business, improving an existing platform, or planning a completely custom solution, SMEvolve is ready to help bring your ideas to life.

---

# Primary Heading

Let's Build Your Next Digital Solution

---

Every business has unique goals and challenges.

Whether you need a professional website, a custom software platform, a mobile application, AI-powered solutions, or business automation, we're ready to help you build technology that supports your growth.

Let's discuss your project and explore the right solution together.

# Design Requirements

The section should:

- Feel visually prominent
- Provide generous whitespace
- Include a subtle background contrast
- Maintain consistency with the Homepage and About CTA sections
- Keep the message concise and easy to scan

Avoid unnecessary visual clutter.

---

# Layout

Recommended structure:

Heading

↓

Supporting text

↓

Primary & Secondary CTA buttons

↓

Optional trust statement

---

# Trust Statement

Optional text:

"No pressure. No obligation. Just a conversation about your project and how we can help."

Keep the tone friendly and reassuring.

---

# Components

Reuse existing CTA components wherever possible.

Suggested components:

- CTA Banner
- Primary Button
- Secondary Button

Avoid creating duplicate CTA implementations.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Ensure:

- Buttons stack cleanly on mobile
- Comfortable spacing on all devices
- Readable typography
- Consistent alignment

---

# Accessibility

The section must:

- Use semantic HTML
- Provide keyboard-accessible buttons
- Include visible focus states
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical

---

# SEO

Although primarily conversion-focused, the supporting text should naturally reinforce SMEvolve's core services without keyword stuffing.

---

# Analytics

Track:

- Quote button clicks
- Consultation button clicks
- WhatsApp clicks
- Contact form visits

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This section is complete when:

- Messaging aligns with SMEvolve's brand voice.
- Primary and secondary CTAs are implemented.
- Responsive behaviour is verified.
- Accessibility standards are met.
- Analytics events are defined.
