# SMEvolve Services Specification
## Part 1 – Services Master Specification

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Services Master Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the overall objectives, content strategy, page structure, design principles, implementation standards, and conversion goals for every service page on the SMEvolve website.

Every service page must follow this document unless a documented exception is approved.

This document serves as the master reference for planning, designing, implementing, and maintaining all service pages.

---

# Business Goal

The Services section exists to educate potential clients, demonstrate SMEvolve's expertise, and convert visitors into qualified enquiries.

Each service page should clearly explain:

- What the service is.
- Who the service is for.
- The business problems it solves.
- How SMEvolve delivers the solution.
- Why businesses should choose SMEvolve.
- How to begin a project.

---

# Primary Objectives

Every service page should:

- Educate visitors.
- Solve business questions.
- Demonstrate expertise.
- Build trust.
- Encourage enquiries.
- Support long-term SEO growth.

# User Intent

Visitors arriving on a service page are usually trying to:

- Understand the service.
- Compare providers.
- Learn about pricing and value.
- Decide whether SMEvolve is the right technology partner.
- Request a quote.

Every page should answer these questions naturally.

# URL Structure

Every service must have its own dedicated URL.

Examples:

```
/services

/services/website-development

/services/ecommerce-development

/services/custom-software

/services/laravel-development

/services/wordpress-development

/services/mobile-app-development

/services/ai-solutions

/services/business-automation

/services/api-development

/services/website-maintenance
```

URLs should remain descriptive and SEO-friendly.

# Conversion Strategy

Primary Conversion

- Get a Free Quote

Secondary Conversions

- Book a Consultation
- Contact Us

---

# Design Principles

Every service page should feel:

- Modern
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Educational
- Easy to scan
- Consistent with the Homepage and About page

---

# Shared Components

Reuse existing Blade components wherever possible.

Examples include:

- Hero
- Feature cards
- Benefit cards
- Technology badges
- FAQ accordion
- CTA banner
- Related service cards
- Buttons

Avoid duplicate markup.

---

# Accessibility

Every service page must:

- Use semantic HTML.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Include meaningful alt text.
- Maintain heading hierarchy.
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical.

---

# SEO Goals

Each page should:

- Target one primary keyword.
- Include related keywords naturally.
- Use descriptive headings.
- Encourage internal linking.
- Prioritize helpful content over keyword density.

---

# Performance

Pages should:

- Load quickly.
- Reuse Blade components.
- Lazy-load images.
- Minimize JavaScript.
- Maintain Lighthouse scores above 90 where practical.

---

# Analytics Goals

Track:

- Quote requests
- CTA clicks
- WhatsApp clicks
- Scroll depth
- FAQ interactions
- Related service clicks

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Services module is complete when:

- Every service page follows this specification.
- Navigation is consistent.
- Components are reusable.
- SEO standards are met.
- Accessibility requirements are satisfied.
- Conversion opportunities are clearly presented.
- Content aligns with the Brand Messaging document.

---

# Revision Policy

This document serves as the master specification for every SMEvolve service page.

Future services should follow these standards unless an approved revision is made.
