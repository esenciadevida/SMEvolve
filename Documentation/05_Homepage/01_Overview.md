# SMEvolve Homepage Specification
## Part 1 – Homepage Overview

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Homepage Master Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the overall purpose, business objectives, user journey, conversion strategy, and implementation rules for the SMEvolve homepage.

Every homepage section must follow this document.

This document is the master reference for homepage development.

---

# Business Goal

The homepage exists to convert visitors into qualified leads.

The primary objective is to encourage visitors to:

- Request a Project Quote
- Contact SMEvolve
- Chat on WhatsApp
- Schedule a Consultation
- Explore Services

The homepage should educate visitors before asking them to take action.

---

# Homepage Objectives

The homepage should:

- Clearly explain what SMEvolve does.
- Establish trust within the first few seconds.
- Demonstrate expertise.
- Showcase services.
- Highlight previous work.
- Present pricing options.
- Remove objections.
- Encourage enquiries.

# Visitor Questions

The homepage should answer these questions quickly.

Within 5 Seconds

- What is SMEvolve?
- What services are offered?
- Is this company trustworthy?

Within 30 Seconds

- Why choose SMEvolve?
- What technologies are used?
- What industries are served?

Within 2 Minutes

- How does the process work?
- What packages are available?
- How do I request a quote?

# Design Principles

The homepage should feel:

- Modern
- Clean
- Premium
- Fast
- Professional

---

# Performance Goals

Largest Contentful Paint

Less than 2.5 seconds

First Contentful Paint

Less than 1.8 seconds

Cumulative Layout Shift

Less than 0.1

Performance should remain a priority throughout implementation.

---

# Accessibility Goals

The homepage should:

- Support keyboard navigation
- Use semantic HTML
- Follow heading hierarchy
- Include descriptive alt text
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical
