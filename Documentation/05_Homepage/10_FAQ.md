# Purpose

This document defines the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section of the SMEvolve homepage.

The FAQ section addresses common questions that potential clients may have before contacting SMEvolve. It helps reduce uncertainty, build trust, improve user experience, and support search engine optimization.

This section is an overview only. A dedicated FAQ page may be created in the future.

# FAQ Items

## 1. What services does SMEvolve provide?

SMEvolve builds business websites, e-commerce platforms, custom software, mobile applications, AI solutions, APIs, and business automation tools tailored to your needs.

---

## 2. How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and requirements. After discussing your project, we'll provide a clear timeline before development begins.

---

## 3. Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. We offer maintenance, updates, and technical support to help keep your solution secure and performing well after launch.

---

## 4. Can you redesign an existing website or application?

Yes. We can modernize existing websites or software by improving design, functionality, performance, and user experience.

---

## 5. How do I get started?

Simply request a free quote or contact us to discuss your project. We'll guide you through the next steps.

# Design Requirements

The FAQ should use an accordion component.

Only one item should be expanded at a time by default.

Animations should be subtle and accessible.

---

# Accessibility

The accordion must:

- Support keyboard navigation.
- Include appropriate ARIA attributes.
- Maintain visible focus states.
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical.

---

# SEO Considerations

The questions should reflect real user intent and be written naturally.

The content should support rich results where applicable using structured data in the future.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The section is complete when:

- All five FAQs are displayed.
- Accordion functionality works correctly.
- The layout is responsive.
- Accessibility requirements are met.
- The CTA is visible.
