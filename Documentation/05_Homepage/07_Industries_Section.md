# SMEvolve Homepage Specification
## Industries We Serve Section

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Homepage Section Specification

---

# Purpose

The Industries We Serve Section shows that SMEvolve understands the unique technology needs of different business sectors.

The purpose is to help visitors identify:

- Whether SMEvolve works with businesses like theirs.
- How technology can solve industry-specific challenges.
- Which solutions may fit their organisation.

---

# Business Goal

The section should:

- Expand SMEvolve's appeal beyond generic technology services.
- Improve industry-specific SEO visibility.
- Help visitors relate SMEvolve's services to their business.
- Increase qualified enquiries.

Primary conversion:

```
Get a Free Quote
```

Secondary conversion:

```
Discuss Your Business Needs
```

---

# User Goal

Visitors should understand:

- SMEvolve works with different industries.
- Solutions can be adapted to specific business requirements.
- Technology recommendations depend on business goals.

# Design Requirements

The section should:

- Make industries easy to identify.
- Avoid limiting SMEvolve to one market.
- Focus on solutions, not just industries.

---

# Visual Direction

Recommended:

- Simple industry icons.
- Clean cards.
- Minimal illustrations.

Avoid:

- Overloaded industry graphics.
- Generic stock photos.
