# SMEvolve Homepage Specification
## Part 12 – Final Call To Action (CTA)

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Homepage Section Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Final Call To Action (CTA) section of the SMEvolve homepage.

The purpose of this section is to provide visitors with one final opportunity to take action after learning about SMEvolve's services, expertise, process, and capabilities.

This section should leave visitors with a clear next step and encourage direct engagement.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the PRD.
3. Read the Information Architecture.
4. Read the Design System.
5. Read the Brand Messaging document.
6. Read the Homepage Master Specification.
7. Follow this document exactly.

Do not use exaggerated marketing language or unrealistic promises.

---

# Business Goal

Increase conversions by encouraging visitors to start a conversation with SMEvolve.

The primary conversion is requesting a project quote.

Secondary conversions include contacting SMEvolve directly or initiating a WhatsApp conversation.

---

# User Goal

Visitors should leave this section knowing:

- SMEvolve is ready to discuss their project.
- Starting the process is simple.
- There is no obligation to make an enquiry.

---

# Section Placement

Position this section after:

- Blog Preview

Position this section before:

- Footer

---

# Section Structure

The section should include:

1. Headline
2. Supporting Message
3. Primary CTA
4. Secondary CTA
5. Optional Trust Note

---

# Headline

Use:

**Ready to Build Something Great?**

---

# Supporting Message

Use:

> Whether you're launching a new business, improving an existing platform, or planning a custom digital solution, we're here to help. Let's discuss your goals and explore the right solution together.

---

# Primary Call To Action

Button Text:

**Get a Free Quote**

Destination:

```
/quote
```

Style:

Primary button.

---

# Secondary Call To Action

Button Text:

**Chat on WhatsApp**

Destination:

Configured WhatsApp link.

Style:

Secondary button.

---

# Optional Trust Note

Display below the buttons:

> No pressure. No obligation. Just a conversation about your project and how we can help.

---

# Design Requirements

The section should feel:

- Welcoming
- Confident
- Professional
- Conversion-focused

Use generous spacing and strong visual hierarchy.

The CTA buttons should be immediately visible without requiring additional interaction.

---

# Accessibility

The section must:

- Use semantic HTML.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Include visible focus states.
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical.

Buttons must have descriptive accessible labels.

---

# SEO Considerations

The content should naturally reinforce SMEvolve's core services and encourage engagement without excessive keyword repetition.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The section is complete when:

- The headline and supporting message are displayed.
- Both CTA buttons are visible and functional.
- The trust note is included.
- The layout is responsive.
- Accessibility requirements are met.
- The design aligns with the Design System.

---

# Revision Policy

Future updates may introduce additional contact options or promotional campaigns, but the primary purpose of this section should remain focused on encouraging qualified enquiries.
