# Purpose

This document defines the Development Process section of the SMEvolve homepage.

The objective of this section is to explain how SMEvolve delivers projects, reduce uncertainty for potential clients, and reinforce confidence through a clear and transparent workflow.

This is an overview section only. Detailed explanations belong on a dedicated "Our Process" page in the future.

# Process Steps

Display the following six steps.

## Step 1 — Discover

Learn about the client's business, goals, audience, and project requirements.

---

## Step 2 — Plan

Define the project scope, timeline, technology stack, and implementation strategy.

---

## Step 3 — Design

Create user-focused interfaces and experiences that align with business objectives.

---

## Step 4 — Build

Develop the solution using modern technologies, clean architecture, and best practices.

---

## Step 5 — Test & Launch

Perform quality assurance, optimize performance, and deploy the project.

---

## Step 6 — Support

Provide maintenance, updates, improvements, and technical assistance after launch.
