# SMEvolve Product Requirements Document
## Product Roadmap

**Project Name:** SMEvolve Website

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Product Roadmap

---

# Purpose

This document defines the planned evolution of the SMEvolve platform.

The roadmap ensures that the initial website is built with future growth in mind while maintaining a clear focus on the current release.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

SMEvolve should grow gradually through well-planned improvements.

Each phase should:

- Deliver business value.
- Improve customer experience.
- Increase revenue opportunities.
- Strengthen the company's technology foundation.

# Roadmap Prioritization

Features should be prioritized based on:

1. Business value
2. Customer impact
3. Revenue potential
4. Technical feasibility
5. Maintenance requirements

---

# Features Not Planned for Initial Release

The following should not delay Version 1.0:

- Client portal
- Mobile applications
- SaaS products
- Advanced AI systems
- Payment processing
- Complex dashboards

These belong to future phases.
