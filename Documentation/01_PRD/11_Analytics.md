# SMEvolve Product Requirements Document

**Project Name:** SMEvolve Website

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Analytics Requirements

---

# Purpose

This document defines the analytics, tracking, reporting, and measurement requirements for the SMEvolve website.

The goal is to understand visitor behaviour, measure marketing effectiveness, optimize conversions, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

# Analytics Philosophy

Analytics should answer:

- Who visits the website?
- Where do visitors come from?
- What services interest them?
- Which pages generate enquiries?
- Where do visitors leave?
- Which marketing channels perform best?

Data should be collected responsibly while respecting user privacy.
