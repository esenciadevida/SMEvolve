# SMEvolve Product Requirements Document
## Executive Summary

**Project Name:** SMEvolve Website

**Domain:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Executive Summary

---

# Purpose

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the business objectives, functional requirements, technical requirements, and long-term vision for the SMEvolve website.

It serves as the primary reference for all planning, design, development, testing, deployment, and future enhancements.

All implementation decisions should align with this document unless an approved revision is made.

---

# Project Overview

SMEvolve is a technology company that helps businesses grow by delivering modern digital solutions.

The website is more than an online brochure—it is the company's primary digital platform for attracting clients, showcasing expertise, generating qualified leads, and supporting future business expansion.

The platform must communicate professionalism, technical expertise, and trust while providing a seamless user experience across all devices.

---

# Vision

To establish SMEvolve as a trusted technology partner for businesses seeking reliable, scalable, and innovative digital solutions.

The website should position SMEvolve as a premium provider of web development, software engineering, AI solutions, and business automation services.

---

# Mission

To help businesses evolve through technology by delivering high-quality digital products that improve efficiency, strengthen online presence, and support sustainable growth.

---

# Business Objectives

The website should:

- Generate qualified leads.
- Increase brand credibility.
- Showcase completed work.
- Clearly communicate available services.
- Educate potential clients.
- Encourage consultation requests.
- Drive WhatsApp enquiries.
- Support long-term organic search growth.
- Provide a scalable platform for future expansion.

---

# Primary Goals

The project aims to:

- Build a fast and professional company website.
- Establish a strong online presence.
- Improve search engine visibility.
- Increase conversion rates.
- Demonstrate technical expertise.
- Create reusable design and development standards.
- Build a maintainable platform that can grow with the business.

---

# Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it achieves measurable improvements in:

- Qualified quote requests.
- WhatsApp enquiries.
- Consultation bookings.
- Organic search traffic.
- Average session duration.
- Conversion rate.
- Returning visitors.
- Search engine rankings for targeted services.

# Project Philosophy

The website should:

- Solve business problems rather than simply display information.
- Educate visitors before selling services.
- Encourage long-term client relationships.
- Reflect high technical standards.
- Remain easy to maintain and expand.

Every feature should contribute to these principles.

# Constraints

The project should:

- Use Laravel 12.
- Use Blade templates.
- Use Tailwind CSS v4.
- Use Alpine.js where appropriate.
- Be mobile-first.
- Be SEO-friendly.
- Meet accessibility standards where practical.
- Be easily maintainable.

---

# Definition of Success

The SMEvolve website is successful when it:

- Clearly communicates the company's value.
- Generates qualified enquiries.
- Demonstrates technical excellence.
- Provides a smooth user experience.
- Performs well across devices.
- Supports future business growth.
