# SMEvolve Product Requirements Document
## Target Audience

**Project Name:** SMEvolve Website

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Target Audience

---

# Purpose

This document defines the intended audience for the SMEvolve website.

Understanding the audience ensures that every design decision, content strategy, feature, and marketing effort addresses real business needs.

---

# Overview

SMEvolve serves organizations that want to use technology to improve business operations, strengthen their online presence, and increase efficiency.

The website should communicate differently to different audience groups while maintaining one consistent brand voice.

---

# Primary Audience

## Small Businesses (SMEs)

Characteristics

- 2–100 employees
- Limited technical knowledge
- Limited IT resources
- Cost-conscious
- Looking for growth

Common Needs

- Professional website
- Online visibility
- Customer enquiries
- Affordable digital solutions
- Reliable technical support

Pain Points

- Outdated websites
- Poor online presence
- Low customer engagement
- Difficulty finding trustworthy developers

---

## Medium-Sized Businesses

Characteristics

- Growing operations
- Existing digital presence
- More complex requirements

Needs

- Business automation
- Internal systems
- Customer portals
- API integrations
- Scalable software

Pain Points

- Multiple disconnected systems
- Manual processes
- Legacy software
- Slow business workflows

---

## Startups

Characteristics

- Fast-moving
- Innovation-focused
- Budget-conscious
- Growth-oriented

Needs

- MVP development
- Fast deployment
- Scalable architecture
- Modern technologies

Pain Points

- Limited funding
- Tight deadlines
- Small technical teams
- Need for rapid iteration

---

## Entrepreneurs

Characteristics

- Individual founders
- New business owners
- Personal brands

Needs

- Business websites
- Landing pages
- Branding support
- Business automation

Pain Points

- Limited technical knowledge
- Limited budget
- Need for quick launch

# Customer Needs

Visitors typically want to:

- Build a new website
- Redesign an existing website
- Develop custom software
- Build an e-commerce platform
- Create a mobile application
- Automate business processes
- Integrate third-party systems

# Customer Expectations

Visitors expect:

- Professional communication
- Transparent pricing
- High-quality work
- Fast response times
- Secure solutions
- Long-term support

The website should reinforce these expectations throughout the user journey.

---

# Communication Strategy

Content should:

- Use clear English.
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
- Explain benefits before features.
- Focus on solving business problems.
- Build trust through clarity and transparency.

---

# User Motivations

Visitors are motivated by:

- Business growth
- Increased revenue
- Operational efficiency
- Better customer experience
- Competitive advantage
- Digital transformation

---

# Common Questions

The website should answer questions such as:

- What services does SMEvolve provide?
- How much will my project cost?
- How long will development take?
- What technologies do you use?
- How do we get started?
- Why should I choose SMEvolve?
- Do you provide ongoing support?
