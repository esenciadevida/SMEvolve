**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Custom Solutions section of the SMEvolve Packages page.

Many businesses require software, automation, AI, or enterprise solutions that cannot be accurately packaged with fixed pricing.

This section explains why these projects require a tailored approach and encourages visitors to begin the Quote Wizard.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the Packages Overview.
2. Read the Pricing Philosophy.
3. Read the Services documentation.
4. Reuse shared Section and Card components.

Avoid displaying fixed prices for custom services.

---

# Business Goals

This section should:

- Position SMEvolve as a technology partner.
- Encourage larger enquiries.
- Explain why bespoke projects require consultation.
- Drive visitors toward the Quote Wizard.

---

# User Goals

Visitors should understand:

- Why some services require a custom quote.
- Which services fall into this category.
- That SMEvolve follows a structured discovery process.
- How to begin that process.
