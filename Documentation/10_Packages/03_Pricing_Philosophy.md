# Purpose

This document defines how SMEvolve communicates its pricing philosophy.

Rather than focusing only on prices, this section explains how projects are estimated, why different engagement models exist, and how clients can choose the most suitable option.

The objective is to educate visitors, build trust, and encourage informed enquiries.

# Factors That Influence Pricing

Explain that pricing depends on factors such as:

- Project scope
- Number of pages
- Required features
- Integrations
- User roles
- Design complexity
- Timeline
- Third-party services
- Maintenance requirements

Avoid displaying exact calculations.

---

# Why Some Projects Require a Quote

Suggested explanation:

Large software projects are highly customised and cannot be accurately priced without understanding your goals, business processes, and technical requirements.

A tailored proposal ensures accurate pricing and realistic delivery expectations.
