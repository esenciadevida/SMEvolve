# SMEvolve Packages Specification
## Part 2 – Page Hero

**Project Name:** SMEvolve

**Website:** https://smevolve.tech

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Packages Page Hero Specification

---

# Purpose

This document defines the hero section for the SMEvolve Packages page.

The hero should introduce visitors to SMEvolve's pricing philosophy, helping them understand that some services are available as predefined packages while others require a tailored proposal.

The messaging should encourage exploration rather than focusing solely on cost.

---

# AI Implementation Instructions

Before implementing this section:

1. Read the AI Constitution.
2. Read the Packages Overview.
3. Read the Design System.
4. Reuse the global Hero component.

Do not create a page-specific hero implementation.

---

# Business Goals

The hero should:

- Introduce the Packages page.
- Explain SMEvolve's pricing approach.
- Reduce pricing uncertainty.
- Encourage visitors to request a quote when appropriate.

---

# User Goals

Visitors should immediately understand:

- SMEvolve offers both standard packages and custom solutions.
- There is a package for businesses of different sizes.
- Complex projects receive tailored quotations.

---

# Layout

The hero should contain:

- Breadcrumb Navigation
- Small Label
- Primary Heading
- Supporting Paragraph
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Supporting Illustration

---

# Small Label

Suggested:

PACKAGES & PRICING

---

# Primary Heading

Suggested:

Flexible Packages for Every Stage of Your Business

Alternative options:

- Solutions Designed Around Your Business
- Transparent Packages. Custom Solutions.
- Choose the Right Solution for Your Business

---

# Supporting Paragraph

Example:

From professional websites to fully customised software and AI-powered business solutions, SMEvolve offers flexible packages and tailored services designed to help your business grow. Explore our options and let us recommend the best fit for your goals.

---

# Primary CTA

Label:

Request a Free Quote

Destination:

/quote

This should remain the primary action.

---

# Secondary CTA

Label:

Compare Packages

Destination:

Comparison Table section on the same page.

---

# Hero Illustration

Use an illustration representing:

- Business growth
- Digital transformation
- Technology solutions
- Collaboration

Avoid generic pricing graphics.

---

# Pricing Notice

Display a short informational panel beneath the hero.

Suggested content:

Some services have transparent package pricing, while larger software, AI, and automation projects receive a personalised quotation based on your requirements.

This prepares visitors for the sections that follow.

---

# Trust Indicators

Display beneath the CTAs.

Examples:

✓ Transparent Pricing

✓ Tailored Solutions

✓ Free Consultation

✓ No Hidden Costs

---

# Visual Design

The hero should feel:

- Modern
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Spacious

Maintain consistency with other page heroes.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop

- Two-column layout
- Text on the left
- Illustration on the right

Mobile

- Single-column layout
- Text first
- Illustration below

Buttons should remain easy to tap.

---

# Accessibility

The hero must:

- Maintain semantic heading structure.
- Include meaningful alternative text for illustrations.
- Provide keyboard-accessible CTA buttons.
- Meet WCAG AA standards where practical.

---

# SEO

The hero should contain the page H1.

Only one H1 should appear on the page.

---

# Analytics

Track:

- Quote CTA clicks.
- Compare Packages clicks.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The hero is complete when:

- Pricing philosophy is clearly introduced.
- CTA buttons are prominent.
- Illustration reinforces the message.
- Trust indicators are displayed.
- Responsive behaviour is verified.
- Accessibility requirements are met.

---

# Revision Policy

Future marketing campaigns may update hero messaging while preserving the overall structure and reusable Hero component.
